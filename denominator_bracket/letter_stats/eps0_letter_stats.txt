7. QUADRATIC LETTER MOVED TO THE LAST INTEGRATION (shuffle)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Script: shuffle_quadratic_last.py
  Since every word has at most one quadratic letter q, write w = u q v
  (u, v linear words, v = v1..vm) and peel v off by shuffle, iterating:
     II[u,q,v1..vm] = sum_{k=0..m} (-1)^k  II[(u sh v_k v_(k-1)..v_1), q] * II[v_(k+1)..v_m]
  e.g.  II[a,b,c,q,d,e] = II[a,b,c,q] II[d,e]
                         - II[(abc sh d), q] II[e]
                         + II[(abc sh e d), q]
  Valid for iterated integrals on any common path and base point (it is a pure
  shuffle identity; no integrability needed).
  Verified exactly (expanding back) on all 2011 quadratic words of the data.

  Result: quadratic part = sum  rational * Const * II[x, q] * II[v]
    q-final words II[x, q]   7090 distinct, length {2:10, 3:54, 4:330, 5:792, 6:5904}
    linear cofactors II[v]   only 37 distinct: length 0 (1), 1 (6), 2 (30)
    distinct pairs           12622
    linear prefix x before q (term counts after rewriting):
      no constant: length 3, 4, 5      Pi^2: length 1, 2, 3
  => q is the outermost integration: each piece is a ONE-FOLD integral
     int dlog(q) * [linear-alphabet function of weight <= 5], times linear
     (two-loop alphabet) functions of weight <= 2.


8. CAN THE REMAINDERS BE WRITTEN IN CLASSICAL POLYLOGARITHMS?  (symbol level)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Script: classical_obstruction.py  ("test" per amplitude, "rank" for the
  dimension of the obstruction space).
  Criterion (Goncharov): a weight-n function is a polynomial in classical
  Li_k (any arguments, products allowed) only if its cobracket components
  delta_{n-k,k}, 2 <= k <= n/2, vanish:
     weight 4: B2^B2      weight 5: B3^B2      weight 6: B4^B2 and B3^B3
  Computed on the symbol (II[a1..an] -> a1 x ... x an), with rho killing
  products; the code returns 0 for Li_n, for products, and nonzero for generic
  words.  The pure functions are F_P = sum r_term(P) II[word], with the rational
  prefactors evaluated at random points P (mod 2^61-1).

  Result: the obstruction is NONZERO in every amplitude, at every weight tested.
    part tested        amplitudes   nonzero   rank of obstruction (all / letters 1-6 only)
    1 * weight 4            84        84            15 / 15
    1 * weight 5            72        72            66 / 66
    1 * weight 6 (4,2)      52        52            24 / 24
    1 * weight 6 (3,3)      52        52            24 / 24
    Pi^2 * weight 4         52        52            23 / 23
  (ranks saturated: a second pass with new random points adds nothing)

  - The obstruction always has a part made only of linear letters 1-6, i.e. the
    two-loop-alphabet part alone is already non-classical.  This needs only that
    the six linear letters are multiplicatively independent.
  - Rank(all letters) = rank(letters 1-6): the quadratic / algebraic components of
    the obstruction are fixed linear functions of the linear-letter components.
    Any combination of the remainders whose linear-letter obstruction vanishes
    has vanishing obstruction altogether.
  => Classical Li_n alone are NOT enough (for any choice of arguments).  At least
     15 (w4), 66 (w5), 24+24 (w6, the two components) independent non-classical
     directions are needed modulo classical functions and products; e.g.
     Li_{2,2}/Li_{3,1} at weight 4, Li_{3,2}-type at weight 5, Li_{3,3}/Li_{4,2}-type
     at weight 6.
