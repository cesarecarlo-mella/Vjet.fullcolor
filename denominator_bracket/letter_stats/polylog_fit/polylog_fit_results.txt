POLYLOGARITHMIC FUNCTION SPACE OF THE eps^0 REMAINDERS  (symbol level, modulo products)
zj.fullcolor / denominator_bracket, 84 amplitudes;  x=s, y=t, z=u, s+t+u=1;  rho = sqrt(-s t u)
==========================================================================================

Method
  Each eps^0 coefficient = sum_i R_i(t,u) F_i with pure functions F_i.  For every amplitude the
  pure functions at each weight are sampled exactly (rational prefactors evaluated at random
  points mod p) and their symbols are projected mod products (rho).  A candidate set of
  functions is "sufficient" if every sampled pure function lies in its span.  Spans are compared
  through random linear sketches (exact arithmetic mod p = 1048573; sketch dimension always
  well above the total rank; negative controls fail as they should).
  Symbols of the candidate functions are computed from Goncharov's recursion for G(a1..an;1),
  checked by the stuffle identity (depth 2) and by integrability on the alphabet (depth 2, 3).

Arguments (arguments_all.txt)
  216 rational: 36 anharmonic orbits, x and 1-x products of letters 1-6, 11-16
  84 algebraic: from S-unit identities with rho, e.g. (su+rho) - rho = su,
                (su+rho) + (st-rho) = s(1-s);  odd letters l7, l8 appear through them
                (the third phase (tu-rho)/(tu+rho) = +-1/(l7 l8) gives nothing new)
  3864 pairs (x1,x2) with x1, x2, x1*x2 admissible;  3600 rational triples with
  x1, x2, x3, x1x2, x2x3, x1x2x3 admissible.

Results
  weight 1-3   all 84 amplitudes:  logs, Li_2, Li_3 of the admissible arguments
               (bare w3 needs only the 9 linear orbits; I*w3 needs the algebraic ones)
  weight 4     all 84 amplitudes (bare and Pi^2 parts):  Li_4 + Li_{2,2}
               non-classical dimension mod Li_4: 31;  sufficient list: w4_Li22_used.txt
  weight 5     all 72 amplitudes:  Li_5 + Li_{3,2} + Li_{2,2,1}
               depth 2 alone leaves 5 directions (C0F0Z1, C2F0Z1, C0F1Z0, C8F0Z1) that
               need the depth-3 function Li_{2,2,1};  non-classical dim mod Li_5: 69
               sufficient list: w5_functions_used.txt (not minimized)
  weight 6     52 amplitudes.  Outside Li_6: 9 directions (amplitudes without l7/l8) + 15 (C1/C7/C9F0Z0).
               * Depth 3 is UNAVOIDABLE (for any arguments): (delta_22 x 1) delta_42 != 0 on all 24
                 directions, and this component vanishes identically for every depth<=2 function
                 (checked: 0 for Li_6, Li_{4,2}, Li_{3,3}).
               * That depth-3 obstruction is reproduced exactly by Li_{2,2,2}(x1,x2,x3) on admissible triples.
               * BUT the full weight-6 symbol is NOT reached by
                   Li_6 (300 args) + all depth-2 Li_{a,b} (3864 pairs)
                   + Li_{2,2,2} (all 3600 rational triples) + the other nine depth-3 compositions (600 triples)
                   + combinations of depth-2 functions whose spurious letter 1-x1x2 cancels
                     (1500 largest product classes: +428 dimensions)
                 (none of the 24 directions is reached, already at the level of delta_42 + delta_33).
               => open: needs depth-3 functions with arguments outside this admissible set
                  (e.g. spurious letters cancelling between depth-3 terms, or algebraic triples).
